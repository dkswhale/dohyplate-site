Pixel Belt

A pixel puzzle with a conveyor belt

Send characters onto a conveyor belt. They clear the cubes of a pixel picture, one color at a time.

Screens

The screenshots show the Korean version.

How to play

  1. Tap the first character in a line. It moves onto the belt around the picture.

  2. The character moves along the belt. When the outermost cube has its color, it shoots and clears that cube.

  3. A character with shots left goes back to a waiting slot. If all slots are full when it comes back, you lose.

When you complete a picture

  • Color comes back to the island, and you get color shards.
  • You can see your pictures again in the album.
  • 20 regions and 300 pictures. Each region adds a new rule.
