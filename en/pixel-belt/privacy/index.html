When a payment on ONE store is completed, ONE store sends a payment notice (purchase number, product code, amount) to our server. The notice has no information that identifies you. The game sends an app open record one time each day (the number of days since the first open). The record has no value that identifies a user, a device, or an installation. The app version adds a leaderboard. The App Store version sends the number of collected pictures to Apple Game Center. The Google Play version sends it to Google Play Games. Apple and Google process this score with your Game Center or Play Games account, and show your rank to your friends. We do not receive this score. The changes are in sections 1 and 3. These items take effect on November 24, 2026. The game sends the score from the first version released after that date.
Notice of change (posted November 17, 2026)
The Toss version adds a friend invite event. For users who use friend invites, we keep invite records on our server (the game user key from Toss, the invite code, the invite relation, and the reward payment records). We do not receive names or contact details. The changes are in the summary and in sections 1 to 4, 6 to 8, and 10. These items take effect on November 24, 2026.
Notice of change (posted November 17, 2026)
The Toss version also sends game usage records to our server. The records do not show whose records they are. It sends Toss point payment results (level, success or failure, failure code) with these records. The records have no value that identifies a user, a device, or an installation. We keep them for 30 days. The changes are in the summary and in sections 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 8, and 10. These items take effect on November 24, 2026. The Toss version sends these records to our server from the first version released after that date.

DOHYPLATE ("we") publishes this policy under Article 30 of the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea. This policy applies to the game "Pixel Belt" ("the game"). This English text is a translation. If the English and Korean texts are different, the Korean text applies.

Summary
  • We do not receive your name, contact details, or device ID. We keep invite records on our server only for users of the Toss version who use friend invites.
  • The app version and the Toss version send game usage records to our server. The records do not show whose records they are. We keep them for 30 days.
  • The game keeps your progress only on your device.
  • Each platform processes payments, ads, leaderboards, and the usage statistics of the Toss version directly.
  • When a payment on ONE store is completed, ONE store sends a payment notice (purchase number, product code, amount) to our server. The notice has no information that identifies you. We keep these records for 5 years.

The game has two versions:

  • Toss version: a mini app that runs inside the Toss app (Apps in Toss)
  • App version: an app from the App Store, Google Play, or ONE store

2. Retention

  • When you delete the app, the progress on your device is deleted. In the Toss version, the Toss app storage policy applies.
  • We keep the game usage records of the app version and the Toss version for 30 days from the day we receive them. Then we delete them.
  • We keep the ONE store payment notice records for 5 years from the day we receive them, to confirm sales. Then we delete them.
  • We keep friend invite records for 1 year from the day the friend invite event ends, to answer payment questions. Then we delete them.
  • We keep server access logs for a maximum of 7 days. Then we delete them.
  • Each platform keeps the information it processes under its own privacy policy.

3. Third parties

We do not give your personal information to third parties. But when we pay a friend invite reward, we send the game user key and the amount to Viva Republica Inc. (Toss). Toss issued this key.

The companies below process information directly under their terms with you.

  • Viva Republica Inc. (Toss): payments, ads, leaderboard, usage statistics, and Toss point payments in the Toss version
  • Apple Inc.: App Store payments and the Game Center leaderboard
  • Google LLC: Google Play payments, the Google Play Games leaderboard, and AdMob ads
  • ONE store Co., Ltd.: ONE store payments

6. Deletion

We delete the game usage records of the app version and the Toss version from the database after 30 days. We delete the ONE store payment notice records from the database after 5 years. Our server deletes friend invite records automatically each day after the period in section 2. We keep no other information. When you delete the app, the operating system deletes the data on your device.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete, or stop the processing of your personal information.

  • To delete the progress on your device, delete the app.
  • For information that a platform processes, contact that platform.
  • The game usage records of the app version and the Toss version have no value that identifies a user. Thus we cannot find, show, or delete the records of one person. All records are deleted after 30 days.
  • The ONE store payment notice records also have no value that identifies a user. If you send us your ONE store purchase number by email, we find the record of that purchase and show it or delete it.
  • To delete your friend invite records, send the "My invite code" from the friend invite sheet to the email below. We delete the records of that code.
  • For other requests, email the privacy officer below. We reply within 10 days.

8. Security

The friend invite server communicates with Toss through a Toss certificate (mTLS). Only the server keeps the certificate and the secret keys. Only the representative has server administrator access. The operating system and the Toss app keep the progress in storage that is separate for each app. The app version and the Toss version send game usage records through an encrypted connection (TLS). The game can add records but cannot read them. Our server stores only the payment notices that have a valid ONE store signature.

9. Automatic collection

The game does not use cookies. The app version uses the advertising ID for ads. To refuse, see section 5.

10. Children

We do not receive the name or contact details of any user, including children. When a user of the Toss version uses friend invites, we keep the friend invite records in section 1. The Toss version sends the game usage records in section 1 to our server. In the app version, our server and the ad SDK process the information in section 1. A parent can ask for deletion of a child's information. Section 7 tells how.
