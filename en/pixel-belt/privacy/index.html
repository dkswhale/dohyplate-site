Pixel Belt Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

DOHYPLATE ("we") publishes this policy under Article 30 of the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea. This policy applies to the game "Pixel Belt" ("the game"). This English text is a translation. If the English and Korean texts are different, the Korean text applies.

Summary
  • We do not operate a server. We do not receive or keep your personal information.
  • The game keeps your progress only on your device.
  • Each platform processes payments, ads, leaderboards, and usage statistics directly.

The game has two versions:

  • Toss version: a mini app that runs inside the Toss app (Apps in Toss)
  • App version: an app from the App Store, Google Play, or ONE store

We do not collect your name, phone number, email, location, contacts, or photos. We do not receive card information. Toss, Apple, Google, and ONE store process payments.

2. Retention

  • When you delete the app, the progress on your device is deleted. In the Toss version, the Toss app storage policy applies.
  • Each platform keeps the information it processes under its own privacy policy.

3. Third parties

We do not give your personal information to third parties. The companies below process information directly under their terms with you.

  • Viva Republica Inc. (Toss): payments, ads, leaderboard, and usage statistics in the Toss version
  • Apple Inc.: App Store payments
  • Google LLC: Google Play payments and AdMob ads
  • ONE store Co., Ltd.: ONE store payments

4. Outsourcing

We do not outsource the processing of personal information.

5. Transfer outside Korea

How to refuseIn your device settings, delete the advertising ID or turn off personalized ads. On iPhone, refuse the app tracking request. You can play the game if you refuse.

6. Deletion

We do not keep personal information. When you delete the app, the operating system deletes the data on your device.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete, or stop the processing of your personal information.

  • To delete the progress on your device, delete the app.
  • For information that a platform processes, contact that platform.
  • For other requests, email the privacy officer below. We reply within 10 days.

8. Security

We do not operate a server or a database. The operating system and the Toss app keep the progress in storage that is separate for each app.

9. Automatic collection

The game does not use cookies. The app version uses the advertising ID for ads. To refuse, see section 5.

10. Children

The game is not for children under 14. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we post the change on this page 7 days before it takes effect. For an important change to your rights, we post it 30 days before.
