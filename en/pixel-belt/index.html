Pixel Belt

A pixel puzzle with a conveyor belt. Each picture you complete brings color back to the island.

  • 20 regions
  • 300 pictures
  • 37 islanders
  • Toss · App Store · Google Play · ONE store

How to play

Three steps

  1. Tap to send

    Tap the first character in a line. It moves onto the belt around the picture. The number on a character shows its shots left.

  2. Clear matching cubes

    The character moves along the belt. When the outermost cube has its color, it shoots and clears that cube. Clear the picture to bring color back to the island.

  3. Collect pictures

    Collect 300 pictures from 20 regions in your album. Each region adds a new rule, such as ice cubes, keys, and tunnels.

Islanders

37 islanders ride the belt

You meet islanders as you complete pictures. Bring them back with color shards, and your characters on the belt take their look.
